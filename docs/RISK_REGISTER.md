| R6  | Unsafe third-party AI handling                                                                    | Medium     | High             | Off by default; consent, scope, human review (M13+)                                                                                                              | Project owner |
| R7  | Mixed currencies corrupt KPIs                                                                     | Low (M3)   | High             | BigInt minor units, decimal strings, per-currency totals; no cross-currency sums without FX (ADR-023)                                                            | Engineering   |

| R21 | Proposal PDF cannot show Arabic text (built-in Latin fonts)                                       | Medium     | Medium           | Print view shows it correctly; embed a Unicode font with RTL shaping (ADR-033)                                                                                   | Engineering   |
| R22 | Production image ships the whole dependency tree (831 MB)                                         | Medium     | Low              | Works and is non-root; prune to API runtime dependencies before production (M11)                                                                                 | Engineering   |

| R28 | Extra work billed without the client's agreement                                                  | Medium     | High             | Budget changes only on client acceptance; extra-work tasks need an accepted change or an audited exception (ADR-051/052)                                         | Project owner |

| R30 | Invoices mistaken for accounting or tax records                                                   | Medium     | High             | "Not accounting software" notice on every invoice screen; CSV export for the accountant; no tax filing features (ADR-054)                                        | Project owner |
| R31 | A payment recorded twice or against the wrong invoice                                             | Medium     | High             | Idempotency key per form, row lock, overpayment refused, reversals instead of edits, audit trail (ADR-055)                                                       | Finance       |
