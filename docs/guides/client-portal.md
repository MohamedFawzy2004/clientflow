# Guide: Client portal

The portal is where you, the agency's client, review proposals, follow your projects, approve work
and see your invoices. You only ever see your own organization's information.

## Getting in

Your agency sends you an invitation link. Open it, enter your name and choose a password. Next time,
sign in at the same address with your email and password.

## Proposals

- **Proposals** lists what the agency has sent you. Open one to read the scope, lines, totals,
  milestones and terms, or download the PDF.
- Choose **Accept**, **Request changes** (tell them what to change), or decline with a reason.
- If the agency sends a new version, earlier versions stay listed so you can compare.

Accepting records your approval of exactly that version (who and when). It is not a formal
electronic signature.

## Projects

- **Projects** shows projects the agency has shared with you: progress, milestones and recent
  updates.
- **Deliverables:** open each one to download the file. Approve it, ask for a revision, or reject it.
  Please say what should change when you do not approve.
- **Change requests:** extra work the agency has priced for you. Accept or decline it; declining
  needs a short reason.
- Comments on deliverables and changes go straight to the team.

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists the invoices the agency has published to you, with what is paid and what is due.
Payments happen outside ClientFlow, for example by bank transfer.

## Privacy

Only the people the agency invited from your organization can see your portal. Signing out ends your
session on that device.
