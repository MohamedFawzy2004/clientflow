# Guide: Sales

You find leads, follow up on time and turn deals into accepted proposals. Salespeople see their own
leads and deals; sales managers see everyone's.

## Leads

- **Leads → Add lead:** name is required; email, phone, company, source and notes are optional. If
  the email or phone matches an existing lead, you are warned, and nothing is merged automatically.
- Set a **next action date** on every lead you are working. **Follow-ups** lists what is due today
  and what is overdue, so nothing slips.
- Log calls, meetings and notes on the lead's timeline. Archive leads you no longer pursue; they can
  be restored.

## Deals and the pipeline

- **Deals → New deal:** pick the lead, a name, the amount and currency. Amounts in different
  currencies are never added together; totals are shown per currency.
- Move a deal between stages from its card or its page. Each deal has a **next step** with a date.
- **Mark lost** asks for a reason. **Mark won** normally happens by itself when the client accepts a
  proposal; a sales manager, Admin or Owner can mark a deal won without one by giving a written
  reason.
- **Export deals** (sales managers, Admins and Owners) downloads a CSV of the deals they can see.

## Proposals

1. From a deal, create a proposal. Add lines from **Services** or type your own, then set the
   discount, tax and expiry date. Totals are calculated for you.
2. **Save draft** as often as you like. Internal notes are never shown to the client.
3. Submit it for approval. A sales manager reviews it: they approve it or return it with a comment.
4. **Send to client** once approved. The client needs a portal account (a sales manager or admin sets
   that up from the lead's page).
5. The client accepts, declines, or uses **Request changes** with a comment. To answer changes,
   start a new version. Each version keeps its own history, and a sent version can never change.

Accepting is an operational approval of scope and price (who, when and exactly which version). It is
not a legal electronic signature.

## After the deal is won

The deal shows a handoff card. A PM, Admin or Owner uses **Convert to project**, and the accepted version
becomes the project's baseline scope.
